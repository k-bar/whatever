# PLAN.md — Warszawa Live MVP

## Vision
One live map of everything social happening in Warsaw; aggregating information from multiple sources on a beautiful interactive map. An easy way to find someone to go with, advertise you're looking for a buddy or a team, connect with people going to concert or sports event, or "friend request" people after the event.

## Who it's for
Warszawa never sleeps, and its inhabitants loose balance of work and responsibilites. We help them find a work life balance, find cool social events and an easy way to make new friends in the city.

They want to go out and want to easily see all their options for interesting places, events and whats on - to pick cool fun things to do, exactly to their taste. Often people who live in Warsaw are new to the city; expats, students or Poles from other cities. This solution is for them.

## The problem
Warsaw's events are split across ticket sellers, city calendars, Meetup groups and Facebook. Each one shows only its own events. Free and recurring events (cultural centres, run clubs, language exchanges) are almost invisible.

## MVP — must have for the demo
1. **Everything on one map:** events from as many Warsaw sources as possible, the city's own **Warsaw Open Data calendar** included, shown inside the city boundary.
2. **Browse by time:** today, this week, this month, with a slider across the next 30 days.
3. **Browse by interest:** clear categories (music, nightlife, theatre, film, exhibitions, meetups, grzybki, study buddies, running, yoga, sports, festivals, markets, culture) and search.
4. **"I'm going, come with me":** say you're going and that you want company. Others ask to join, and you accept or reject them.
5. **Sharing a public invitation** Maybe you're having a picnic, going to a concert or even welcoming strangers to your and want to invite cool people. You must also be able to see their profile and accept/reject them - possible Tinder swiping mechanic.
6. **Profiles** Each user has a profile which others can see and its their responsibility for it to be appealing to others if they want to be accepted to their event. Share photos, interests, maybe web links to public Instagram, Github, Facebook etc.. People can get in touch and find each other.
7. **Trust in the data:** an "APIs I'm plugged in to" list showing **reliable** information, where every event comes from and how many events each source gives.
8. **Anonymous browsing** New users can also see the data without creating an account

## What makes us different
- **Extensive information** A deep aggregation of "all" the sources.
- **Right now:** what's starting in the next few hours that you can reach quickly by public transport.
- **Half-empty tonight:** good shows tonight with plenty of seats left.
- **Squads:** go in a small group of 3–4 instead of meeting one stranger.
